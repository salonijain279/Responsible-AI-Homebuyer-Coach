# Designing a Responsible AI Coach for First-Time Homebuyers

A product-strategy and governance case for an explainable AI coach that helps first-time homebuyers plan savings, compare scenarios, and understand uncertainty without replacing licensed financial advice.

## Customer problem

First-time buyers often face fragmented program information, changing rates and prices, hidden ownership costs, and uncertainty about how savings choices affect readiness. A useful AI experience must simplify those decisions while keeping assumptions, conflicts, and limitations visible.

## Product concept

The coach supports a multi-stage journey:

1. establish an affordable target range;
2. compare down-payment and savings scenarios;
3. monitor progress and update assumptions;
4. evaluate buy-now versus wait scenarios;
5. prepare questions for a qualified mortgage professional.

## Trust by design

- transparent calculations and cited inputs;
- confidence ranges rather than false precision;
- user overrides with an explanation of downstream effects;
- product-conflict disclosure;
- fairness monitoring and human escalation;
- strict separation between education and regulated advice.

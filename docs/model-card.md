# Model card

## Intended use

Educational scenario modelling for adults voluntarily planning a future home purchase.

## Inputs

User-provided income range, savings, recurring obligations, target geography, time horizon, down-payment preference, and risk tolerance. Production use would require explicit consent and documented data retention.

## Outputs

- scenario ranges;
- estimated savings trajectory;
- assumption and sensitivity summary;
- confidence/uncertainty indicators;
- recommended questions for a qualified professional.

## Prohibited use

The output must not determine credit eligibility, interest rates, underwriting, pricing, marketing exclusion, or access to housing programs.

## Key limitations

- Market rates, taxes, insurance, and prices can change materially.
- User inputs may be incomplete or inaccurate.
- Historical patterns may not transfer across places or groups.
- Behavioral recommendations can create unequal burdens.
- Confidence language may be misunderstood as certainty.

## Human oversight

Escalate conflicts, high-risk affordability scenarios, complaints, protected-class concerns, and unclear program eligibility to trained staff. Preserve the input, output, model version, and explanation shown to the user.

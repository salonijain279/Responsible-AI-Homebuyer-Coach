# Risk register

| Risk | Example harm | Control | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| False precision | Buyer treats a forecast as guaranteed | Ranges, sensitivity analysis, visible assumptions | Calibration and comprehension testing |
| Product steering | Coach favors an affiliated product | Conflict disclosure and neutral comparison rules | Recommendation mix by product and segment |
| Proxy discrimination | Geography or finances act as protected-class proxies | Feature review, fairness testing, prohibited-use controls | Outcome and override gaps |
| Privacy leakage | Sensitive financial information is exposed | Data minimization, encryption, access controls, deletion | Access logs and incident alerts |
| Automation bias | User follows AI despite warning signs | Human handoff and uncertainty prompts | Handoff and reversal rates |
| Stale information | Programs or rates are outdated | Timestamped sources and expiry rules | Source freshness SLA |
| Accessibility failure | Key explanation is unusable | WCAG-aligned design and assistive testing | Accessibility defect tracking |

# Product brief

## Goal

Help a first-time buyer move from uncertainty to an informed plan while preserving control and access to qualified human advice.

## User needs

- Understand a realistic purchase range.
- See the full cost of ownership, not only the down payment.
- Compare savings and timing trade-offs.
- Know which assumptions drive a recommendation.
- Adjust personal priorities without losing the audit trail.

## Experience principles

1. Progressive disclosure: start simple, expose detail on demand.
2. Scenario comparison: show at least three viable paths.
3. Transparent math: define each input, source, and formula.
4. User agency: allow overrides, correction, export, and deletion.
5. Human handoff: make escalation timely and context-rich.

## Non-goals

- Approving or denying credit
- Predicting an individual's protected characteristics
- Replacing a loan officer, housing counselor, attorney, or financial adviser
- Presenting market forecasts as guarantees
